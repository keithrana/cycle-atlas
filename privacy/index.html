Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CycleWise AU ("the app", "we", "us") helps you track your menstrual cycle, ovulation, and pregnancy. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and secure your account, and (if you sign in with Google) the identifier Google provides to verify who you are.
  • Cycle and pregnancy data: dates you enter — such as your last period start date, cycle length, period length, and last menstrual period — used solely to compute and display your cycle phases, fertile window, and pregnancy week information within the app.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifiers beyond what's needed to keep you signed in.

How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the app's core features: showing your calendar, cycle phase, fertile window, and pregnancy week details, and syncing that information across devices you sign into. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Tracking

CycleWise AU does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Who can see your data

Only you. Your cycle and pregnancy data is tied to your account and is not visible to other users, and we do not manually access it except as needed to maintain the service (for example, responding to a support request you send us) or if required by law.

Where it's stored

Your account and cycle data are stored on servers operated by our hosting provider (Railway) with a managed PostgreSQL database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. If you sign in with Google, authentication is verified directly with Google; we never see or store your Google password.

Your choices

  • You can edit or clear your cycle and pregnancy dates at any time from within the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below — we'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

CycleWise AU is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
