Privacy Policy

Draft. This page is a plain-language starting point. A lawyer must review it before LookAtU launches, because rules for children's data differ by country.

What we collect today

This website collects only the email address and phone type (Android, iPhone or both) that you type into the waitlist form.

Why we collect it

We use it only to tell you when LookAtU opens. We do not sell it. We do not use it for anything else.

Who sees it

The LookAtU team, and the service that stores the waitlist for us.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email at any time. Every email we send will have a way to unsubscribe.

The LookAtU app (coming later)

The app will handle location, activity and messages of children. Before launch we will publish full details here: what is collected, how it is protected, how long it is kept, and how a parent can delete it. The child will always be able to see that the app is installed.
