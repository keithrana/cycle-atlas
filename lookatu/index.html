Built on trust, not secrets

Safety works best when families talk about it.

  • Your child knows. The app is always visible on their phone. LookAtU is never hidden.
  • Private by design. Data is encrypted and only the parent account can see it.
  • You stay in charge. Choose what to watch and turn features on or off at any time.
  • Follows the law. Rules about monitoring differ by country and a child's age. We check them before we launch anywhere.
