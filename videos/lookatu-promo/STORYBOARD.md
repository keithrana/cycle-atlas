## Video direction

Daisy Days picture-book look: cream and pastel grounds, 3px charcoal outlines, hard offset shadows,
hand-drawn ornaments (daisy, star, sun, cloud) filling the corners. Warm, reassuring, never scary.
Motion is soft and bouncy (back.out, power3.out), each piece lands when the voice names it. Phone
mock-ups are invented generic screens with no real brand logos. Captions sit in the bottom band.
