## Intent

Promo for "lookatu", an Android and iOS child-safety app. Parents control how
much their kids use the phone (time limits, app control) and can track location
only when needed. Story shape: start with a worried parent, then lookatu brings
calm and safety. Warm, reassuring, trustworthy. Not scary, not spying.
